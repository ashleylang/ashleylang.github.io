About

I am an ecosystem ecologist studying plant-soil-microbial interactions. Using a variety of approaches, I explore links between fungal communities and ecosystem carbon storage to predict how Earth's carbon cycle will respond to change.

When I'm not at my desk, you can find me growing flowers, making art, sewing quilts, and wrangling my three young daughters.

Art + Science

I carve nature-themed stamps for art prints and fabric, and sew science-themed quilts.

Data Quilt Mockups

I worked with students from Virginia Tech to develop an app to help design quilts that represent ecological data. This app translates long-term data from Hubbard Brook Experimental Forest into color-coded squares so you can make a quilt showing the last 50 years of rainfall volume, stream pH, or whatever else your heart desires.
